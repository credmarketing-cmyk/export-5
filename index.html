Getting started
01How fast can we get started?+

Live within 24 hours. Walk us through your calls, your greeting, service area and rules, and we set CLARA up around the way you already work. Most teams start with one agent, often after-hours and overflow calls, and add more from there.

02Do we have to replace our field-service or accounting software?+

No. CLARA works inside the software you already run. It reads what it needs and writes the results back, so your system stays the source of truth.

03Can my office keep answering during the day?+

Yes. Many teams start CLARA on after-hours and overflow, and hand it more of the line when they’re ready.

04Is CLARA built for our trade?+

CLARA is built for commercial service contractors: fire and life safety, HVAC, electrical and mechanical. It speaks NFPA inspections, deficiencies, service agreements and invoices on terms, and it learns your business’s own technical information.

Trust and control
05Who decides what CLARA is allowed to do?+

You do. Permissions are set by person and role, and sensitive actions are off by default. CLARA can’t change settings, prices or records you haven’t opened to it.

06What if CLARA gets something wrong?+

You choose which actions need a human yes: discounts, payment plans, schedule changes, anything you like. Every call, message and update is logged with what CLARA did and why, and anything unusual goes to your team.

07What happens on an after-hours emergency?+

You set the rules before go-live: which calls count as emergencies and who gets them. CLARA follows those rules and routes urgent calls to your on-call team.

Working with your customers and team
08Will my customers know they’re talking to AI?+

Our customers tell us their callers are happy to talk to CLARA: the call gets answered and the job gets booked. CLARA sounds natural and follows your rules for what to say.

09Is CLARA here to replace my office team?+

No. It takes the repetitive follow-through off them: the calls, confirmations and chasing nobody has time for. Your team handles the exceptions and the relationships.

10How does CLARA chase overdue invoices without upsetting customers?+

It calls on the schedule and in the tone you set, records the promise to pay, and calls back on the promised date. Anything sensitive goes to your team.
